Through Patterns

Nicosia, Cyprus

Exhibited at 'Resilient Bodies, Resonant Voices' (Group Exhibition)

The work addresses the lived experiences of women within social, cultural, and patriarchal structures, focusing on states   of vulnerability, endurance, and resistance that often remain unseen.

Light operates as a central metaphor for awareness and visibility. When it passes through the surface, it activates the work by revealing what is usually concealed: emotional traces, fragility, and strength.

Each visual layer carries its own meaning while remaining inseparable from the others. The material surface, the imagery, and the light function together, allowing personal and collective narratives to overlap. Through this layering, the work creates a space where multiple readings coexist, and where vulnerability and resilience are understood not as opposites, but as interconnected states

Untying the patterns

Here, the ornament represents cultural codes into which stereotypes, gender roles, and restrictions have been woven for generations.

Meaningless laws

The papyrus symbolizes the laws or principles that no longer function, yet still shape our present.

The illusion of responsibility

We are taught to feel responsible for breaking cycles of oppression ourselves, yet the system is built in a way that makes escape nearly impossible.

Stepping on the past

Women continue to move forward, yet they inevitably step on the sharp fragments of of the past: trauma, memory, and the long-term consequences of patriarchy.
