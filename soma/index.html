OVERVIEW

Sōma is a conceptual ambient sleep companion and charging dock spin-off for the Oura Ring, designed to transform cold biometric sleep data into a warm, screen-free morning ritual.

I was deeply inspired by the original Oura brand and thoroughly studied their identity system before developing this ecosystem from the ground up, crafting the branding, tactile packaging, pitch deck, and web experience to translate their sleep science into a physical sanctuary.

CLIENT (SELF-INITIATED PROJECT)

OURA x SOMA

DELIVERABLES

Brand Identity, Packaging, Pitch Deck, Motion Design, AI Visuals, Art Direction

YEAR

2026

RESEARCH & PROCESS

Fascinated by the concept of the Oura Ring and how effortlessly it tracks the body’s natural rhythms, I explored the science of circadian awakening and realized how much gentler, healthier, and more pleasant mornings could feel if the ring worked in harmony with an ambient bedside light—guiding you out of sleep naturally at the exact moment your body is ready.

Translating this idea into a visual system, I combined Oura’s refined editorial aesthetic with tactile material studies—using generative AI workflows to prototype the frosted glass hardware and warm 2700K lighting, then shaping the complete narrative across packaging, web, and pitch deck design.

BRAND IDENTITY & NAMING

I named the product Sōma, the Ancient Greek word for the living body, and built a visual system around Oura's clean typography and calm-tech tone. Using generative AI workflows, I prototyped the frosted matte lamp, subtle time display,and warm 2700K sunrise glow.

TACTILE PACKAGING SYSTEM

To make the unboxing feel quiet and grounded, I designed rigid boxes in stone-beige uncoated cotton paperboard with soft letterpress embossing. Inside, a molded cream pulp tray holds the lamp and ring dock without plastic inserts.

PITCH DECK, WEB & MOTION

I structured the product story into a clean Swiss-grid pitch deck and web experience, pairing editorial product photography with clear hardware specs. I also produced short motion loops showing the gradual morning light shift and box details

THE RESULT

A complete hardware concept spanning brand identity, physical packaging, web design, pitch slides, and motion assets. Combining restrained editorial typography with realistic AI product visualization turned an abstract wellness idea into a tangible, cohesive brand ecosystem.
