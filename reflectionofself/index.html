Solo exhibition 'Reflection of Self'

Nicosia, Cyprus, Rustem's Bookshop & Art Gallery - historic cultural hub established in 1937

The exhibition explores reflection not only as a physical phenomenon but as a psychological process through which identity is continuously shaped and redefined. It examines how individuals come to understand themselves through memory, relationships, personal beliefs, and encounters with others.
Rather than presenting reflection as a stable image, the project approaches it as a shifting and often fragmented experience, revealing the complex ways in which self-perception evolves over time.

Drawing on themes of memory, vulnerability, resilience, and transformation, the exhibition creates an immersive environment that encourages introspection. Through symbolic imagery, material experimentation, and spatial experience, visitors are invited to navigate the space between inner psychological realities and the external world.

The artworks do not offer fixed narratives or definitive answers; instead, they function as reflective surfaces through which viewers may recognize their own emotions, memories, and personal histories. In this way, Reflection of Self transforms the gallery into a space for contemplation, where identity emerges as an ongoing dialogue between perception, experience, and self-awareness.
